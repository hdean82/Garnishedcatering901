GOOD TO KNOW

A few things you might wonder

Questions before reaching out? Here's where to start.

Where does Garnished Catering serve?

We're based in the Memphis area and serve the Mid-South. Reach out with your event location so we can discuss what's possible.

How do I get catering information or a quote?

Send us your event date, location, estimated guest count and what you're celebrating. We'll discuss the details and available options with you. Email Garnished Catering.

How do I access my client account?

Choose Client Login above and sign in with your invited email address. Once your account is connected, you can view invoices shared with you, save copies and manage your login details.

I'm part of the Garnished team. Where do I sign in?

Choose Team Login to open the private Studio. Team access requires permission from the workspace owner; clients cannot access internal Studio records.
